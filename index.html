The tallest address in Tbilisi

Rising more than 147 metres above Vake, Axis Towers is the tallest building in the capital — one tower in white stone, one in dark glass, each floor turning two degrees around its core, in opposite directions.

A landmark of Georgian architecture and engineering — and, since 2020, the first building in the region with World Trade Center status.
