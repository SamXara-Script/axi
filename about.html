The Towers

Engineering a twist

Axis Towers began with a group of architects and an idea that seemed impossible: two towers, one of stone and one of glass, turning around their own axes in opposite directions.

Our story

A seemingly impossible project

The project started when a group of architects imagined a large-scale building that Tbilisi had never seen. Today Axis Towers is a flagship of Georgian architecture and engineering, and the tallest building in the capital.

Its two towers combine five worlds — a Class A business center, the five-star Pullman hotel, residences, a shopping center and places to eat, drink and unwind.

Architecture

Two degrees per floor

Each floor turns two degrees around the core relative to the one below — clockwise in one tower, counter-clockwise in the other — so the pair spiral away from each other as they rise.

Stone and glass

One tower is clad in white stone and holds the residences; the other is wrapped in dark glass and holds the business center and the hotel.
